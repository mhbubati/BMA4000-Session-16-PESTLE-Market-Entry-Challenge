PESTLE Market Entry Challenge 🌍

Ten markets. Six pillars. One question: do you have the judgement to know when to Enter, Investigate, or Do Not Enter?

📋 How This Works

You'll face ten market scenarios, each with six PESTLE signals. Some are positive ✅, some negative ⚠️ — and each carries a strength: Strong, Moderate, or Weak.

Your task is not to count positives and negatives. It's to weigh them. A single strong negative can outweigh five weak positives. When the signals are genuinely mixed, the honest answer is Investigate Further.

Correct Enter/Do Not Enter → +£1,000. Correct Investigate → +£800. Wrong → −£500.

🔑 Key Takeaways

  • Strength beats number — one strong signal can outweigh five weak ones.
  • Counting is not analysis — weighing the evidence is the skill.
  • "Investigate Further" is a real answer — uncertainty is not an excuse for a rushed decision.
  • PESTLE factors interact — a positive in one pillar cannot cancel a strong negative in another.
  • Wealth ≠ opportunity — a rich market with legal or cultural barriers can still fail.
